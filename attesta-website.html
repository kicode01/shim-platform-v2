From sign-in to sign-off

One QR gets someone into the record. A second, separate one lets anyone confirm what's in it — after the event, and after that.

1

The organizer builds the certificate

Pick a ready-made template or upload your own layout. Define the roles you're issuing for — participant, speaker, committee, exhibitor, member — in one event batch.

2

The attendee scans in at the event

A single attendance QR at the door confirms who actually showed up and unlocks their certificate for claiming — no manual request-and-issue afterward.

3

The certificate gets claimed

Members have it linked automatically to their membership ID and visible in their portal. Guests claim it with just a name and email — no account required.

4

Anyone can verify it

A separate verification QR — printed on the certificate itself — lets an employer, another institution, or the public confirm it's authentic and unaltered.

Built for everyone in the room

Organizers, members, and guests each get exactly the access they need — nothing more.

Organizers

  • Customize a template or upload your own layout
  • Generate certificates and IDs in bulk from a CSV list
  • Issue more than one certificate to a recipient per event
  • Revoke a certificate issued in error

Members

  • One membership ID linking every certificate you've earned
  • Search past events and download from your portal anytime
  • Signature authentication required before any download

Guests and verifiers

  • Claim a certificate with just a name and email
  • No account needed to claim or to verify
  • Scan any certificate's code for an instant answer
"Verification should mean something today, not just at issue."
