# Design Context

## Design Context

### Users

Two coexisting audiences on one product:

- **Organizers / issuers (HQ dashboard).** Event staff and programme administrators working at a desk. They issue credentials, manage templates, run check-in, and audit the ledger. They need density, speed, and at-a-glance confidence that the system is in sync. They are not designers; they read the UI as an instrument panel.
- **Attendees (wallet).** Participants holding credentials they earned. Their dominant context is **a phone, in the field, at a venue door, under time pressure, often in poor light**. The job to be done is *prove I belong here, right now* — show the check-in pass, then show the credential. Browsing the full history is a secondary, calmer activity done afterwards.

Emotional target: **quiet authority**. The attendee should feel the credential is official and trustworthy without feeling surveilled or gamified. The organizer should feel the ledger is real and under control.

### Brand Personality

Three words: **precise, institutional, understated.**

Voice is terse and technical-but-human — mono micro-labels, no marketing exclamation, no emoji. The product's self-confidence comes from structure and hairline precision, not from colour or motion.

References: Swiss/International-Typographic systems grids; airline and bank instrument panels; the restraint of a well-built design system rather than a landing page.

Anti-references (explicit): generic AI-SaaS visual tropes — gradient icon chips, pastel tinted stat cards, soft shadows on everything, `rounded-2xl` blobs, stock Lucide hero icons, floating glassmorphism. Also explicitly not crypto/Web3 "vault" styling (neon, hex-hash aesthetics, NFT gallery framing).

### Aesthetic Direction

Light theme, zinc-based neutral palette, black as the accent ink.

**The organizer page is the canonical signature; the wallet must be the same system, not a cousin.** Shared vocabulary, extracted from `src/app/dashboard/DashboardClient.tsx`:

- **Page frame** — `max-w-7xl mx-auto w-full px-8 py-6`
- **Section title** — `text-3xl font-bold text-zinc-700 mb-1` with `text-zinc-500 text-sm font-medium` subtitle beneath
- **Card** — `bg-white border border-zinc-200 rounded-xl shadow-sm`
- **Card header** — `p-6 border-b border-zinc-200`
- **Metric ribbon** — the single most identifying element: `bg-zinc-200 border border-zinc-200 rounded-xl shadow-sm grid gap-[1px] overflow-hidden` where the 1px gap *is* the border between cells; each cell `relative p-5 bg-white flex flex-col justify-start hover:bg-zinc-50/80 transition-colors overflow-hidden group`
- **Ghost watermark** — an oversized, very faint icon inside ribbon cells: `absolute -right-2 -bottom-3 opacity-[0.06] group-hover:opacity-[0.09]`
- **Metric label / value** — `text-sm font-medium text-zinc-500` / `text-3xl font-bold text-zinc-800 tracking-tight`
- **Badge idiom** — `text-[10px] font-bold tracking-widest uppercase text-zinc-500 bg-zinc-100 border border-zinc-200/80 px-3 py-1.5 rounded-md shadow-[inset_0_1px_3px_rgba(0,0,0,0.08),0_1px_0_rgba(255,255,255,1)]`, usually paired with a small inline icon ("LEDGER SYNCED")
- **Logo** — isometric "S": three parallelogram faces with two knocked-out slashes, `viewBox="0 0 256 256"`, `currentColor` (`public/logo-mono.svg`, `public/icon.svg`)
- **Wordmark** — `shim.attendee` / `shim.organizer`, `font-black tracking-[-0.08em] lowercase`

Palette: `#18181b` (ink), `#71717a` (muted), `#e4e4e7` (hairline), `#fafafa` (page), `#ffffff` (surface). Emerald is reserved for *valid* state signalling only.

### Design Principles

1. **One system, two surfaces.** The wallet is the organizer dashboard re-pitched for one person and one column — same card, ribbon, label, and badge grammar. A user crossing between surfaces should never sense a seam.
2. **Structure carries the brand.** Precision from hairlines, the 1px-gap grid, mono micro-labels, and the isometric mark — never from gradients, tinted chips, or stock icons. If it looks like a template, it's wrong.
3. **Optimise for the door.** Speed-to-proof is the top priority: the check-in pass and the credential count must be reachable in one glance on a small screen, legible in bad light, with generous tap targets.
4. **Colour is information, not decoration.** Neutrals do all layout work; emerald means valid and nothing else; per-credential ink appears only as a small controlled accent.
5. **Quiet authority.** Every element should read as official and deliberate. No playfulness that undercuts trust, no gamification, no decorative motion.
